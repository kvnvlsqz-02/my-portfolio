Attend Ease

2025Mobile App

Background

Attend Ease is a specialized mobile attendance application built for the students of a single university department featuring four academic programs. By combining classroom QR code scanning with instant facial verification, the app eliminates manual roll calls and buddy-punching, allowing students to securely log their attendance in seconds.

Problem

University attendance tracking is historically flawed. Manual roll calls consume valuable lecture time, while standard digital portals are slow to navigate on mobile devices and vulnerable to proxy attendance. The core challenge of this redesign was to craft a hyper-fast, secure logging loop that students could complete while rushing into a classroom, while also providing them with a stress-free way to monitor their absence limits.

Solution

I completely overhauled the experience by designing a true mobile-first dashboard that prioritizes speed and clarity. Key UI/UX improvements included replacing the dense data tables with a clean, personalized profile header to instantly confirm user identity. To streamline navigation, I introduced a central grid of large, accessible quick-action buttons (Take Attendance, Leave, Activity Log, Class Schedule), entirely eliminating the need to hunt for tiny text links. Finally, I transformed the messy attendance logs into a highly scannable "Recent Attendance" list, utilizing clean card layouts and color-coded status badges for immediate cognitive recognition.
