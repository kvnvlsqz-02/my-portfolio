Flavor Fusion

2024Flavor Fusion

Background

Flavor Fusion is a recipe discovery and sharing platform where people can upload their own recipes, browse thousands of dishes from the community, and swap their own twists with other food lovers. In this redesign, I turned it into a warm, minimal, easy-to-use digital cookbook.

Problem

The original interface was cluttered and hard to read. Dark, semi-transparent overlays on the food photos made the text difficult to make out. Recipe pages were overwhelming, with long stacked lists of ingredients and instructions, no clear hierarchy, and main buttons that got lost in the density.

Solution

I concentrated on simplifying the journey and improving clarity. A toggle between “Ingredients” and “Instructions” cuts down on scrolling and mental load. A bold, high-contrast “Cook Now” button stays pinned to the bottom of the recipe page, so the next step is always obvious. To fix legibility, I removed the dark photo overlays and set crisp sans-serif text on clean white cards. Finally, I replaced the clunky header menus with a persistent, easily recognizable bottom navigation bar, which makes browsing much easier.
